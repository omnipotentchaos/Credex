# Go-To-Market Strategy — CredexAudit

> A free AI spend audit tool by [Credex](https://credex.rocks)

## Positioning

CredexAudit is **"Mint for AI tool spend"** — a free, no-signup audit that tells engineering teams exactly where they're overspending on Cursor, Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, and similar tools. The output is a shareable report with dollar-specific recommendations.

The strategic purpose: every audit naturally surfaces Credex as the solution. When CredexAudit says "you could save $120/mo on Cursor credits," the CTA leads straight to the Credex marketplace. The tool is the top of the funnel; Credex is the conversion.

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Engineering managers and tech leads at seed-to-Series B startups (10–100 employees) who manage a growing AI tool budget but haven't audited it yet. These buyers have purchasing authority, feel cost pressure from founders, and are actively looking for ways to optimize without sacrificing developer productivity.

**Secondary:** Solo developers and indie hackers paying for 3+ AI subscriptions personally, spending $50–$200/month and wondering if they're on the right plans.

## Channel Strategy

### 1. Twitter/X (Primary — organic)
Post the audit results page as a screenshot thread: "We just ran CredexAudit on a 15-person eng team. Found $2,400/year in waste across 4 tools." Developer Twitter loves data-dense visuals. Target hashtags: #buildinpublic, #devtools, #startups. Engage with threads about "Cursor vs Copilot" and "AI tool costs" — these are people self-selecting into our audience.

### 2. Reddit (Primary — organic)
Post audit case studies (anonymized) to r/startups, r/SideProject, and r/ExperiencedDevs. Reddit rewards genuine value-first content. A post titled "I built a free tool to audit AI spend — here's what I found running it on 50 teams" would perform well. Include real numbers, acknowledge limitations.

### 3. Product Hunt Launch (Week 2)
Launch CredexAudit as a free tool on Product Hunt. The "free audit" framing performs well on PH — it's immediately actionable and the results page is inherently shareable. Target a Tuesday or Wednesday launch for maximum visibility.

### 4. LinkedIn (Secondary)
Write a post targeting CTOs and VP Engs: "The average 20-person startup overspends $400/month on AI tools. Here's a free way to check." LinkedIn's algorithm rewards text posts with specific numbers. The professional audience here converts better for Credex's enterprise sales.

### 5. Newsletter Partnerships
Reach out to developer-focused newsletters (TLDR, Bytes, Changelog) for a mention. The pitch: "Free tool, no signup, saves your readers money" — easy for newsletter editors to share because it's genuinely useful.

## Conversion Funnel

```
Landing page → Audit form → Results page → Lead capture → Credex CTA
                                  ↕                           ↕
                           Share on social            Credex marketplace
```

The key insight: value delivery happens **before** lead capture. The user sees their full savings report first, then we ask for an email. This post-value sequencing dramatically increases both email capture rates (they've already seen the value) and Credex marketplace clicks (they now believe the savings are real).

## Why This Works for Credex

CredexAudit creates **zero-cost demand generation**. Every audit that finds savings above $100/mo is a pre-qualified Credex lead — the user has already quantified their pain and seen that Credex credits can solve it. This is warmer than any cold outreach because the user self-served their own proof of need.
